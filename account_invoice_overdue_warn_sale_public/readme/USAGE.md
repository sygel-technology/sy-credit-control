To use this module:

1. Create and post an unpaid customer invoice with a due date in the past.
2. Create or open a sales order for the customer related to the overdue invoice.
3. Log in as an accounting user and verify that the overdue warning is displayed on the sales order with a link to the overdue invoices.
4. Log in as an internal user without accounting permissions and verify that the overdue warning is displayed on the sales order, but the link to the overdue invoices is not available.
